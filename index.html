Research

Master's thesis

University of Trieste / SISSA 2026

A Variational Approach to the Shallow-Water Equations via an Energy Minimization Process

Supervisor: Prof. Stefano Bianchini, SISSA Trieste

In my Master's thesis I constructed a global weak solution to the shallow-water system in a rotating frame through a variational time-discretization scheme. At each step, the scheme determines the evolution by minimizing the mechanical energy of the system.

The construction is inspired by the optimal-transport approach developed by Gangbo and Westdickenberg for the system of isentropic Euler equations, and combines tools from partial differential equations, calculus of variations and optimal transport.
